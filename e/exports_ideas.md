## Pharmaceuticals

For Pharmaceuticals exports Plan for **Ayurvedic products**, **nutraceuticals**, **herbal extracts**, and **private-label wellness products** as they often provide a lower entry barrier than prescription pharmaceutical exports, which require extensive regulatory approvals

### OEM/ODM Private label Manufacturing Certifications:
- GMP-certified production
- ISO 22000 / HACCP
- US FDA facility registration (for food supplements)
- Organic certifications
- Private labeling
- Custom formulations
- Amazon-ready packaging
- UK and EU-compliant labeling
  
### Regions to explore:
- US, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, France, Netherland, Italy, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia, South Africa

### High demand products
- Ashwagandha, Curcumin/Turmeric, Shilajit, Moringa, Probiotics, Mushroom supplements, Herbal teas, Joint health, Essential oils and Herbal Extracts

## Tea / Spices

### Essential certifications

| Certification | Required For | Importance |
|---------------|-------------|------------|
| FSSAI License | India | Mandatory for manufacturing/export |
| IEC (Import Export Code) | India | Mandatory for exporting |
| GST Registration | India | Business compliance |
| APEDA Registration | India | Export promotion and market access |
| Spice Board Registration (CRES) | Spices Export | Recommended / often required |
| Tea Board Registration | Tea Export | Recommended for tea exporters |
