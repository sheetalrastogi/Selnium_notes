### Three Amigos Collaboration

Three Amigos is an Agile/BDD (Behavior-Driven Development) collaboration technique where three key perspectives work together to define and validate requirements before development begins.

## Purpose

The purpose of Three Amigos collaboration is to create a shared understanding of requirements before development begins.

## Roles

| Role | Focus | Key Question |
|------|--------|-------------|
| Business Representative (Product Owner / Business Analyst) | Business requirements and value | "What problem are we solving?" |
| Developer | Technical implementation | "How will we build it?" |
| Tester / QA | Validation and quality risks | "How do we verify it works?" |


### Benefits

- Reduces misunderstandings
- Identifies requirement gaps early
- Improves test coverage
- Prevents defects before development
- Encourages collaboration across teams
- Produces clear acceptance criteria
- Supports Shift-Left Testing and BDD practices
