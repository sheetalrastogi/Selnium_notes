6. Resolve your unresolved problem

midnight take two meethe paan. keep one in on chauraaha and one in your hand.  Pray "Chauraaha" ka god and beg him to "Come with you - asking to solve your un-resolved problem"

do this without fear and come back home.

bring the second paan home and keep it in mandir.  Eat it in morning.
