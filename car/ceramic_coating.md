Step 1:
do a thorough cleaning of car and use degreaser, anti-flackers to remove debris, greese and hard water sclaes on car

Step 2. once clean - do a claying (to dissolve paint ingested dust)

Step 3. Mask the car chrome, plastics and logos 

Step 4.use woolpad or forced foam pad and rubbing compound to do paint smoothning

Step 5. use soft foam pad and soft compound - do a second round of polishing

Step 6.  use clean microfiber and isopropyl alcohol to clean polished surface
IPA cleaning is must before ceramic coating.

Step 7. do first layer of ceramic coating eg. X1 Blue (Ceramic lacquer)
once done, use clean microfiber to clean excess of ceramic coating.

Step 8. do second layer of ceramic coating eg. Y2 lotfe (Crystal shield)
remove excess by wiping with clean microfibre

Step 9. do a third layer of ceramic coating eg. z5 coating (activator for ceramic polish)

Step 10.  keep the car in shaded region for 4-5 hrs for curing
this is to create a permanent bond between ceramic coating and paint for long lasting effect.

Step 11.  Use Mag 4 for alloy wheel ceramic coating

Step 12: use C6 for glass ceramic coating.
