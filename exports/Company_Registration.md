### US
For an Indian founder setting up a U.S. entity to support:
- Shopify sales
- Amazon US marketplace
- Tote bag exports
- Private-label products

**Wyoming LLC** + **Northwest Registered Agent** remains one of the cleaner and more predictable-cost options because the recurring registered-agent fee is clearly published at $125/year and includes the first year when you form through them.

## Europe 
**Estonia Company** (Best Entry Point Into EU)
```text
India Exporter
      +
 Estonia OÜ
      +
 Wise Business
      +
 EU Customers
```

For your planned tote bag and sustainable products export business, I would start with **UK Ltd** via **1st Formations** if your initial target buyers are in the UK and Europe, and **Wyoming LLC** via **Northwest** if you intend to focus on Amazon US and U.S. wholesalers.

For many non-EU founders, Estonia remains the easiest route because of:
- e-Residency program
- Fully digital company management
- Access to EU customers
- Good for SaaS, consulting, ecommerce and export trading entities

Official: https://www.e-resident.gov.ee/
