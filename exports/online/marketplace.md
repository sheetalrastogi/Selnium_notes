**When VAT Registration May NOT Be Required Initially**
Scenario: Merchant Fufilled (MFN)

```text
Customer in Germany places order
↓
You ship directly from India
↓
No inventory stored in EU
```
In this model, **local EU VAT registration may not be immediately required**, although **customs**, **import VAT**, **IOSS**, and other **tax rules** can still apply depending on the shipment structure and value. 


**In Your Specific Case (Canvas Tote Bags)**

If you plan to:

```text
Manufacture in Idia
↓
Ship inventory to Amazon Germany warehouse
↓
Sell across Europe
```
then **German VAT registration** will typically be required before or shortly after inventory begins being stored in Germany, regardless of sales volume. 

A common setup for Indian exporters is:

```text
Amazon Europe Seller Account
+ German VAT
+ EORI Number
+ Germany FBA
+ EFN for other EU countries
```

This is often the simplest and most cost-effective way to enter the EU market before expanding to Pan-European FBA.
