n most cases, you do not need to obtain GOTS or OEKO‑TEX certification yourself if you are acting as a trader/exporter and sourcing from a certified manufacturer.

Scenario 1: You're a Trader/Exporter (Most Common Initially)

You buy bags from a factory and export them under your company name.

✅ Ask the manufacturer for:

GOTS Certificate
OEKO‑TEX Standard 100 Certificate
Scope Certificate (for GOTS)
Transaction Certificate (TC) when required
Certification body's details

The manufacturer must hold the certification.

You can market the products as certified only if the manufacturer's certification covers the product and supply chain requirements are met.
