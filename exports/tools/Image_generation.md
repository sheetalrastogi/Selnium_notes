**AI image-generation models that create high-quality images from text prompts.**

Midjourney and FLUX are AI image-generation models that create high-quality images from text prompts. They are widely used for product catalogues, lifestyle shots, advertising creatives, packaging concepts, and e-commerce visuals.

## 1. Midjourney
Website: https://www.midjourney.com

**Strengths**
- Premium-looking product renders
- Luxury and lifestyle photography
- Interior design scenes
- Catalog cover images
- Marketing banners

Example Prompt:
```text
Luxury matte black bathroom accessories collection,
towel rail, toilet roll holder, soap dispenser,
glass shelf, premium hotel bathroom,
European interior design,
studio lighting,
photorealistic,
8k
```

## 2.FLUX
Website: https://flux.ai (and available through platforms such as Fal.ai, Replicate, ComfyUI and others)

Example prompt:

```text
Brushed stainless steel towel rail,
isolated product shot,
white background,
premium bathroom accessories,
commercial product photography,
shadow under product,
catalogue style,
high detail
```
