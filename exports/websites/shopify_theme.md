**Artistic - Art & Craft Store theme**

```text
The Artistic - Art & Craft Store theme does not require any special Razorpay integration. In Shopify, payment gateways are connected at the store level, so Razorpay will work with the Artistic theme just like any other Shopify theme.

Step 1: Create & Activate Razorpay Account
Sign up for a Razorpay merchant account.
Complete KYC verification.
Wait for account activation.
Step 2: Install Razorpay in Shopify
Login to Shopify Admin.
Go to Settings → Payments.
Under Additional payment methods, click Add payment method.
Search for All-in-one Razorpay Payment Gateway (formerly "1Razorpay").
Click Install and Activate.
Step 3: Connect Razorpay Account
After installation, select I am an existing user.
Login using your Razorpay Account Owner credentials.
Authorize the Shopify store connection.
Razorpay will appear as an active payment gateway.
Step 4: Enable Payment Methods

Inside Razorpay Dashboard, enable:

UPI
Credit Cards
Debit Cards
Net Banking
Wallets
EMI (optional)
International Payments (optional)
Step 5: Test the Integration
Shopify Admin → Settings → Payments.
Open Razorpay settings.
Enable Test Mode.
Place a test order on your store.
Confirm successful payment processing.
For an Artistic / Art & Craft Store

Since you are selling handcrafted products, pottery, ceramics, tote bags, or export items, I recommend enabling:

✅ UPI
 ✅ Cards
 ✅ Net Banking
 ✅ International Cards
 ✅ Multi-currency checkout (if targeting UK/EU/US customers)
 ✅ COD (for Indian market)

Important for Indian Shopify Stores
Shopify Payments is not available in India, so Indian merchants typically use third-party gateways such as Razorpay.
If you plan to export products internationally, request International Payment Gateway activation in Razorpay and submit the required export compliance documents.
Will You Need Coding?

No. Razorpay integrates through Shopify's Payments settings and works with the Artistic theme without editing theme code.

For your planned export-focused Artistic craft store, I would recommend a complete setup with:

Razorpay
COD
WhatsApp Chat
Multi-currency pricing
Shiprocket
GST invoices
SEO settings
Export-ready product pages

which can be configured without custom development.
```
