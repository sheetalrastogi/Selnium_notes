This diagram represents a Security Operations Center (SOC) Operational Framework that aligns cybersecurity operations with business objectives, governance, risk management, compliance, security monitoring, threat response, and continuous improvement.

## 1. Architectural Overview

The framework is organized into five logical layers:
```text
Business Requirements
        ▼
Security Intelligence Layer
        ▼
SOC Operations Layer
        ▼
Client Infrastructure Layer
        ▼
Governance & Continuous Improvement
```

The design follows a top-down approach where business requirements drive security strategy, which in turn governs operational monitoring and incident response activities.
