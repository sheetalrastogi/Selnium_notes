### Beyond T-Shaped Engineering, some of the **Skill profile models** used to describe how engineers develop depth, breadth, leadership, and cross-functional capabilities.


# Engineering Skill Profile Models

| Model | Shape | Meaning | Best For |
|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| I-Shaped | I | Deep expertise in one domain only | Specialists |
| T-Shaped | T | Deep expertise + broad cross-functional knowledge | Modern Engineers |
| V-Shaped | V | Deep expertise in two related domains | Full-Stack Specialists |
| Pi-Shaped (π) | π | Deep expertise in two domains + broad knowledge | Architects |
| M-Shaped | M | Multiple deep specializations | Principal Engineers |
| Comb-Shaped | Ш | Several areas of expertise at varying depths | Technical Leaders |
| E-Shaped | E | Expertise + Experience + Exploration + Execution + Education | Engineering Managers |
| X-Shaped | X | Technical expertise + leadership/influence | Engineering Leaders |
| Tree-Shaped | Tree | Broad foundation with multiple growing specialties | Long-term Career Growth |
| A-Shaped | A | Technical depth + strong collaboration | Agile Teams |
| H-Shaped | H | Deep skill + ability to connect teams/functions | Program & Solution Architects |
| U-Shaped | U | Broad expertise across many domains with moderate depth | Generalists |
| Key-Shaped | Key | Domain expertise combined with business knowledge | Product/Business-Focused Engineers |
