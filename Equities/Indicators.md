# Technical Indicators

| Full Indicator Name | What It Means | Bullish Signal |
|--------------------|---------------|---------------|
| Relative Strength Index (RSI-14) | Measures overbought/oversold conditions (0–100 scale). | Above 50, strong above 60 |
| Awesome Oscillator (AO) | Measures market momentum using moving averages. | Above 0 |
| Stochastic RSI (Stochastic Relative Strength Index) | RSI applied to a stochastic formula; more sensitive than RSI. | Above 20 and rising |
| Stochastic Oscillator | Compares closing price with recent trading range. | %K crossing above %D |
| Momentum Indicator | Measures the rate of price change. | Above 100 |
| Williams %R (Williams Percent Range) | Similar to Stochastic; values range from 0 to -100. | Rising above -80 |
| Commodity Channel Index (CCI-20) | Identifies cyclical trends and overbought/oversold levels. | Above 100 |
| Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD 12,26,9) | Trend-following momentum indicator based on EMAs. | MACD crossing above Signal Line |
| Ultimate Oscillator | Combines short-, medium-, and long-term momentum into a single indicator. | Above 50 |

# Fundamental Ratios

| Fundamental Ratio | What It Means | Bullish / Healthy Signal |
|-------------------|---------------|--------------------------|
| PE Ratio (Price-to-Earnings) | How much investors are willing to pay for ₹1 of earnings. | PE between 10–25 for mature businesses and below industry average |
| Price to Book Value (P/B Ratio) | Measures market value relative to net assets. | Generally < 3 for non-financial companies |
| EV to EBIT | Measures company value relative to operating profit. | Lower than industry average, preferably < 15 |
| EV to EBITDA | Popular valuation metric that includes debt. | < 8 Attractive, 8–15 Fair, > 15 Expensive |
| EV to Capital Employed | Measures how the market values invested capital. | ROCE growth higher than EV/Capital Employed growth |
| EV to Sales | Useful when profits are volatile. | < 2 Attractive, 2–5 Reasonable, > 5 Expensive |
| ROCE (Return on Capital Employed) | Measures how efficiently management uses capital. | > 15% Good, > 20% Excellent |
| ROE (Return on Equity) | Measures profit generated from shareholder funds. | > 15% Good, > 20% Excellent |

# Bullish Fundamental Screening Criteria

| Metric | Bullish Threshold |
|----------|------------------|
| PE Ratio | Below industry average |
| P/B Ratio | < 3 (non-financials) |
| EV/EBITDA | < 12 |
| EV/Sales | < 3 |
| ROCE | > 15% |
| ROE | > 15% |
| Revenue Growth | > 10% CAGR |
| Profit Growth | > 15% CAGR |
| Debt-to-Equity | < 0.5 |
| Promoter Holding | Stable or increasing |

---

# Strong Fundamental Bullish Setup

A stock is considered fundamentally strong when most of the following conditions are met:

- ✅ ROCE > 20%
- ✅ ROE > 18%
- ✅ EV/EBITDA < 12
- ✅ PE below sector average
- ✅ Debt-to-Equity < 0.5
- ✅ Revenue Growth > 10% CAGR
- ✅ Profit Growth > 15% CAGR
- ✅ Promoter Holding stable or increasing

**Interpretation:** Such companies are typically profitable, efficiently managed, reasonably valued, and exhibit sustainable growth, making them attractive long-term investment candidates.
