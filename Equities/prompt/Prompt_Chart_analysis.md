Act as a professional technical analyst and price action trader.

Analyze the attached daily stock chart screenshot and provide a detailed assessment based ONLY on the visible chart data.

Tasks:

1. Chart Structure Analysis
   - Identify the overall trend (Bullish, Bearish, Sideways).
   - Determine whether the stock is making:
     - Higher Highs / Higher Lows
     - Lower Highs / Lower Lows
     - Consolidation or Range Bound movement
   - Identify any breakout or breakdown patterns.

   - List the top 5 resistance levels with explanation.

3. Price Action Interpretation
   - Examine recent candles and explain:
     - Bullish or bearish engulfing patterns

4. Volume Analysis
   - Compare volume on up days versus down days.
   - Identify accumulation or distribution signs.

5. Demand and Supply Zones
   - Mark demand zones where buyers emerged aggressively.
   - Mark supply zones where sellers entered strongly.
   - Rank the zones by strength.

6. Trend Strength Assessment
   - Evaluate the probability of:
     - Continuation of current trend
     - Pullback
     - Reversal
   - Give confidence level (%) for each scenario.

7. Trading Scenarios
   Create three scenarios:

   Bullish Case:
   - Entry zone
   - Stop loss
   - Targets T1, T2, T3

   Neutral Case:
   - Trading range
   - Trigger levels

   Bearish Case:
   - Breakdown level
   - Stop loss
   - Downside targets

8. Risk Analysis
   - Identify nearby resistance overhead.
   - Mention signs of exhaustion or weakness.
   - Point out any risk of bull trap or bear trap.

9. Key Levels Summary
   Present a table:

   Level | Type | Strength | Reason
   ------|------|----------|-------
   xxxx  | Support | Strong | Previous swing low
   xxxx  | Resistance | Moderate | Multiple rejections

10. Final Verdict
    Provide:
    - Short-term view (1-5 trading days)
    - Medium-term view (2-8 weeks)
    - Most likely next move
    - Probability score (Bullish %, Bearish %, Sideways %)

Important:
- Use exact price levels visible on the chart.
- Prioritize swing highs, swing lows, breakout points, and high-volume zones.
- Explain the reasoning behind every support and resistance level.
- If the screenshot includes indicators (EMA, SMA, RSI, MACD, VWAP, Bollinger Bands), incorporate them into the analysis.
