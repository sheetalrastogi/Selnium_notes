## 3. Security Analyst should be proficient in the following core activities

Learn to speak "Bold points" below

**Security Governance & Risk Management**
- Perform cybersecurity risk assessments.
- Support security policies, standards, and procedures.
- Ensure compliance with frameworks and regulations (GDPR, HIPAA, PCI-DSS).
- Manage identity and access controls (IAM, MFA, SSO, least privilege).

**Vulnerability Management**
- Conduct vulnerability scanning and assessment.
- Prioritize vulnerabilities based on business risk and exploitability.
- Track remediation activities and patch management.
- Maintain a vulnerability knowledge base and risk register.
- Validate security fixes through testing.

**Network Security Monitoring**
- Analyze network traffic and security logs.
- Perform port and service discovery using tools such as Nmap.
- Review firewall, VPN, IDS, and IPS configurations.
- Identify network-based attacks and indicators of compromise.
- Investigate suspicious communications and unauthorized access attempts.

**Incident Detection & Response**
- Monitor security events and alerts.
- Investigate phishing, malware, and intrusion incidents.
- Execute incident response activities including identification, containment, eradication, and recovery.
- Support root-cause analysis and lessons-learned reviews.
- Develop and tune IDS/IPS detection rules (e.g., Snort)


**Threat Analysis & Cyber Attack Investigation**
- Analyze attack techniques including phishing, social engineering, MITM, DoS/DDoS, and credential attacks.
- Understand attacker behavior using the Cyber Kill Chain framework.
- Identify indicators of attack and compromise.
- Assess emerging threats and their business impact


**Web Application Security**
- Assess web applications against OWASP Top 10 risks.
- Identify SQL Injection, XSS, and other application vulnerabilities.
- Review secure coding practices and mitigation controls.
- Validate effectiveness of application security controls.

**Malware Analysis & Security Operations**
- Detect and investigate malware infections.
- Analyze ransomware, trojans, spyware, worms, and rootkits.
- Support endpoint protection and malware remediation.
- Monitor security tools and antivirus/antimalware solutions.

**Security Monitoring & Forensics**
- Investigate email security incidents and phishing attempts.
- Perform basic email header and forensic analysis.
- Analyze logs from systems, applications, and networks.
- Correlate security events to identify potential threats.
