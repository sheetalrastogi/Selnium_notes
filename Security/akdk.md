Simple Summary

This slide presents a NIST-based cybersecurity consulting framework. Organizations are assessed across the five NIST domains:

Identify → Protect → Detect → Respond → Recover

The assessment results are used to build a technology roadmap, close security gaps, improve maturity scores, achieve compliance or certification, and establish a cycle of continuous cybersecurity improvement.
